Customer call: Harbor & Finch Freight, ops lead Joon Park, 12 March

Maya: How long did classification take before you started using Tidewater?

Joon: Honestly about eleven minutes per shipment. Someone has to look up the HS code, check the exemptions, then paste it into three different systems. We were doing around 300 shipments a week with six people.

Maya: And now?

Joon: About two minutes. The suggested code comes up, we confirm it, and it files. Last week the six of us did 340 shipments and nobody stayed late.

Maya: What about mistakes?

Joon: Our first-pass rejection rate was 14 percent. Last quarter it was 4 percent. Fewer rejections means fewer calls from the port authority, which honestly is the part my team likes most.

Maya: Did you trust the suggestions straight away?

Joon: No. The first week we checked every one by hand. What won us over was that it shows the three past shipments each code was matched from, so I can see why it picked it.
